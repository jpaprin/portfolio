About Me

I bring a broad range of life and clinical experience to my work as a therapist. After earning a Master of Architecture from Harvard University and practicing for 15 years, I transitioned into human psychology to pursue deeper interpersonal connections.

Clinical Background & Training

I earned my Psy.D. at William James College and developed a rigorous clinical foundation across institutions including Massachusetts General Hospital, Massachusetts Institute for Psychoanalysis, and Harvard Vanguard.
